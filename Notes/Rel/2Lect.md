## Church Lingo
    At least four different Groups were among the 
    early Christians:
        Jewish Christian Adoptionists
        Marcionite Christians
        Gnosticism
        Proto-Orthodox Christians
    

### New Testament and Old Testament
    When it comes to reading the NT in church, most services read little chunks
    of scripture instead of the full text, from beginning to end. The gospel of 
    Matthew, Romans, Jude, none of these were written to just be picked from, but
    instead, to be digested in full.
