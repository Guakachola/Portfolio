### Biblical Manuscripts
    ca 1400 - 1469, the printing press was invented by Johannes Gensfleisch zur Laden zum Gutenberg. it was oil-based, used wooden printing presses, and metal movable types. Before then, books were fully written by hand on codex's, then even further before, they were written in scrolls. These were written in scriptoriums, considering most people couldn't read or write, these were written by professionals.
    Some important manuscripts include the Greate Isaiah Scroll from Qumran, which is dated around the 1st or 2nd century B.C.E.. It is important to note that scribal errors occur and are real, because men are imperfect. The Aleppo Codex is one of the oldest complete manuscript of the OT, however the Torah was ripped out of it, so it is not included in the copy we have today. It was written around 930 C.E.. Another manuscript is codex Leningradensis (L). This is the absolute oldest OT manuscript (since it has the Torah), and is dated to 1008 C.E.. The translation BHS( the actual Hebrew Bible is written off of this ) renders the text of L. One of the oldest (imcomplete) manuscript is P.52, which is John 18:31-33. This is literally the earliest extant NT canonical record.
    Another huge Codex is Codex Alexandrinus, which is whole NT and Septuagint, dated to 5th Century C.E.. Minuscle is another greek codex written in the 16th Century.
