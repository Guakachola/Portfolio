## Representing the American People
	Members of Congress must be responsive to their constituents, which is the aera from which they are elected. The constitution created a **bicameral** legislature: a legislative body consisting of two chambers.

Congress comes in two chambers, House and Senate. House members are more attuned to local groups than Senators are, since there are more house members per state than Senators. 

## Representation

Members of Congress interpret their jobs in: 
	Delegate model: Act and Vote in accordance with the view of their constituents.
	Trustee: Act and vote in their own thoughts, trusted by those that vote for them.
Descriptive representation have the same racial, gender ... as their constituents, being a very literal form of representation of the population.

Another way for people to represent their population are Caucuses. There are a lot for each and every race, gender, ideology, ethnic, and so on.

### Composition

As the elections go on, we get closer and closer to a 'true' representation of the U.S., in race and gender. But now, there is a increase in numbers, but it is not close to equal to the population. 

**Representatives as Agents**

None the less, representatives should act to further their constituents' views and their interests. Most of the time, this happens on social media, through email, websites, blogs, and actual social media. Many districts has a couple top issues that representatives fight for, because that are usually the most important issues to the constituents.

## Electoral Connections

### Incumbency

Incumbency is holing the political office for which one is running. These guys can provide constituent services and pork barrel (appropriations made by legislators for local projects). They also have superior access to campaign funds.

Majority leader: Elected by majority party; second in the party hierarchy following the Speaker. Most powerful person in the Senate.

Minority leader: elected leader of the minority party
